# Crawl Heuristics

## Use single-page scrape when
- the page is clearly specified
- the task is to summarize or compare one page

## Use focused crawl when
- the domain matters but the exact page is not known
- trust centers, resource centers, or newsroom pages are likely targets

## Prune aggressively when
- pages are off-topic
- crawl fan-out becomes broad
- low-authority sources overwhelm primary sources

## Always record
- why the target was chosen
- what was not crawled and why
- what gaps remain
